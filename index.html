Welcome to the wild side

Where the jungle meets the water

Tucked beside Yala, Sri Lanka's most famous wildlife region, Twin Lake Yala is a peaceful retreat where mirror-still lakes reflect misty hills, and elephants wander the green shores at dawn.

Whether you come for the safaris, the birdlife or simply to unwind with a sunset over the water, this is the Sri Lanka you imagined.

Things to do

Unforgettable experiences

From heart-pounding safaris to quiet moments on the water.

🐘

Elephant Encounters

Watch wild herds graze and bathe along the lake shores, right from a safe, respectful distance.

🐆

Jeep Safaris

Explore Yala's trails at first light for a chance to spot leopards, sloth bears and crocodiles.

🦜

Birdwatching

Peacocks, painted storks, kingfishers and eagles: the lakes are a paradise for birders.

🌅

Sunrise & Sunset

Golden light over misty hills and glassy water. Bring a camera, you will not regret it.
